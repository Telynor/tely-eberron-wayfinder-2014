This is an unofficial Foundry conversion prepared for Telynor from the supplied *Wayfinder’s Guide to Eberron* 2018 campaign prototype. Dungeons & Dragons, Eberron, the source text and source illustrations belong to Wizards of the Coast and their respective creators. This module is not an official Wizards of the Coast, D&D Beyond or Foundry product.

Artwork provenance is recorded in `source/art-provenance.json`. The Clawfoot asset is a cutout edit of the linked D&D Beyond illustration, with rider/scenery removed; it is not represented as an unmodified official illustration. Other source illustrations were extracted from the supplied book. Fallback icons are referenced from the Foundry installation, not redistributed.

The automation code and conversion data are identified separately from the original source material. No license to the original book or art is granted by this repository.
